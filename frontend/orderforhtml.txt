## 📌 Recommended Order (Based on Your Pages)

### **1. General & Entry Pages**

1. `index.html` → Landing/home page.
2. `about.html` → About the platform (accessible from navbar).
3. `contact.html` → Contact/support form.
4. `404.html` → Error page (design later, simple).

---

### **2. Authentication**

5. `login.html` → Common login for patients, doctors, admins.
6. `register.html` → New patient registration (doctor/admins can be handled by admin later).

---

### **3. Patient Journey**

7. `patient-dashboard.html` → Main hub for patient after login.
8. `book-appointment.html` → Core feature for patients.
9. `my-appointments.html` → View/cancel upcoming appointments.
10. `patient-profile.html` → Update personal details & health info.

---

### **4. Doctor Journey**

11. `doctor-dashboard.html` → Doctor’s hub after login.
12. `doctor-appointments.html` → List of today’s patients/appointments.
13. `doctor-patient-records.html` → View patient history & add notes/prescriptions.

---

### **5. Admin Journey**

14. `admin-dashboard.html` → Central hub for admin.
15. `admin-hospitals.html` → Manage hospital records.
16. `admin-doctors.html` → Approve/manage doctors.
17. `admin-patients.html` → Manage patients.

---

### **6. Utility**

18. `settings.html` → Common settings (account, theme, notifications).

---

## ✅ Suggested Build Order

To keep it smooth, build in this order:

1. **index → about → contact → 404** (general shell & navbar).
2. **login → register** (auth flow).
3. **patient pages** (dashboard → book → my appointments → profile).
4. **doctor pages** (dashboard → appointments → patient records).
5. **admin pages** (dashboard → hospitals → doctors → patients).
6. **settings** (last, polish).
